    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty about 4 out of 10. Do the stages in order: the story first (and the one possibly-missable meditation in Chapter 1), then side strands, activities, collectibles, crime and money, and finally the 100% cleanup. The checklist under Progress only appears after the story ends.

      Save smart: the game has no mission replay and 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter. Cheats unlock only after the story and they disable trophies and saving, so leave them off on a trophy run.

      Missable trophies

      Good news: almost nothing can be missed. After the story ends, Leonida stays open for free roam, every side character tied to a trophy is still around, and the 100% checklist opens up. Only one trophy needs care during the story.

      Safe to leave for later

      Every other trophy can be earned after the final mission in free roam: all side strands, all collectibles, all activities, all robberies and all counts. Optional missions can expire and a few fences can disappear for good, but none of those blocks a trophy.
